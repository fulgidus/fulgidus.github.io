The use of gen-AI ranges from autocorrect to hardcore slop. There's everything in between, and I'm not innocent. This page is here to say where I stand, where I got it wrong, and where I've decided to act.

## A tool, not a replacement

AI, in my opinion, is fine when it produces real value and helps people grow their own skills.  
An assistive tool, like many others that existed before. Not a replacement for my brain.

Roughly:
- **Autocomplete:** fine.
- **PRs written by an agent:** it depends. If you do it because your workload forces you to, I don't judge (we all live under the performative yoke of modern capitalism). If it's laziness, I start to take issue.
- **Translation:** fine, if you really don't know the language... I use it to deal with french bureaucracy, because I don't speak french and I have no choice. But if I could, I'd speak/write myself, in a language I know. And when it comes to articles, blogs or social media, I always wonder why anyone would try to communicate with an audience whose culture and language they don't know.
- **Generative writing:** if you want to communicate something you care about, the effort of expressing yourself has to be invested. It works like proof-of-stake: [*no stake, no value*](https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/aidr-meaning).
- **Articles generated from scratch:** no. The idea makes me feel sick, and I'm ashamed of having done it. If a person can't communicate, better they don't express themselves at all than dilute their idea in the mechanized mediocrity that comes out of an LLM: there are already enough opinions in the world, and I much prefer an ugly article to one mediated by OpenAI's latest batch of stolen training data.
- **Generated images:** I thought they were harmless, I changed my mind. Better to give work to someone on Fiverr, or make a mess in GIMP.

## It's not a god, it's T9 on steroids

AI is sold as a new god. *It's T9 on steroids.*

Under the pretext that we should treat it like a living thing, those who sell it avoid paying for the damage it does: industrial accidents, hacking, environmental destruction, or worse.

The idea that it's inevitable, and that we must adapt, is blatantly false (and stems from rape culture, where the rich, the privileged and the powerful implicitly have the right to exploit everyone else). We stopped gene editing on human embryos, didn't we? It's just a matter of no longer making exceptions for these technofascists, wanna-be feudal lords of the human intellect.

Moreover, I find the Machiavellian logic that would justify millions of deaths today in the name of a hypothetical future utopia *intolerable and immoral*.

## It's class war

This is a new form of systemic violence, one that attacks the very spirit of humanity. It robs the "99%" of the last bargaining chip they had left: technical, professional, and artistic skill.

It's a large-scale attack between social classes, and it must be treated as the declaration of war it is.

## My contradictions

Today the short-sighted, stupid and capitalist climate forces me to use AI heavily, both in the products we build at work and in my professional life.

Code is an emanation of my will. Sometimes, for lack of time, I'm reduced to mechanizing its production, this site included. I'm not proud of it, and I'm still coming to terms with it. To survive, I take refuge in electronics in my free time: there, you still need a skillset that AI can't touch, except at exorbitant cost.

Many of the older posts on this blog aren't "halal": you'll find them marked with [#made-with-ai](/tags/made-with-ai). I won't go into detail about how much AI is in each one. Then again, the generated covers speak for themselves.

## My craft is no longer for sale

They want to turn the sculptor, the carpenter, the watchmaker we were into a generalist worker, a line cook, a cog that's easy to threaten because it's easy to replace?  
Fine. If my skills aren't wanted, I'll keep them for my personal projects and my private life.  
The soul, the love and the craft with which I built, imagined and created are, from now on, no longer for sale, barring exceptions decided on a case-by-case basis.
